## Garbage bag situation in Kyoto City

| Type                                                            | Color       | 45L  | 30L  | 20L  | 10L   | 5L  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ----- | --- |
| Burnable waste                                                  | Yellow translucent | ¥45 | ¥30 | ¥20 | ¥10  | ¥5 |
| Recyclable waste  <br>(cans, bottles, PET bottles)<br>(plastics) | Clear       | ¥22 | ¥15 | ¥10 | ¥5  | —  |

The price and capacity shown are per designated bag. Burnable waste bags are sold in **sets of 10**, and recyclable waste bags are sold in **sets of 5**. ([Source](https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kankyo/page/0000000179.html))

When buying, say to the clerk: **One set of designated 10-liter burnable waste bags, please.**
